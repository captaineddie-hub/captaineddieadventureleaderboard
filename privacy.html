Short Track Showdown Privacy

Last updated November 7, 2026

What we store

  • A random player ID created when you first open the game. It is not linked to your name or phone.
  • Your game progress: driver name, car number and paint, cash, upgrades, stats, achievements and league results.
  • Your email address, only if you choose to save your career with email or Google. It is used to sign you in and nothing else.

Who can see it

Your driver name, car number, colors and league results are visible to other players in leagues you join. Your career details and email are private to your account.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, or send marketing email. The game doesn't use your location or contacts. Tilt steering reads your phone's motion sensor on your phone only, and that data is never sent anywhere.

Where it's kept

Game data is stored with our hosting and database provider (Lovable Cloud, built on Supabase). Google sign-in is handled by Google.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and game data, email superwebagent@gmail.com from the address on your account, or tell us your driver name and league code. We'll remove it within 30 days.
